

# Coffee Store Sales & Market Expansion Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales, customer, product, and city-level data for a coffee retail business using SQL.

The objective is to identify high-performing cities, understand customer purchasing patterns, evaluate product demand, and uncover potential market expansion opportunities through data-driven analysis.

The analysis was performed using a relational database consisting of multiple interconnected tables and business-focused SQL queries.

---

## Business Problem

A growing coffee retail business wants to understand:

- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- Which coffee products drive the most sales?
- How customer spending varies across locations?
- Which cities offer the best market expansion opportunities?
- How sales performance changes over time?
- Which cities provide the best balance between revenue potential and operating costs?

The goal is to support strategic business decisions related to expansion, product planning, and customer growth.

## Key Findings

### Revenue Leaders
- Pune generated the highest overall sales revenue.
- Chennai and Bangalore ranked among the strongest performing markets.

### Customer Spending
- Customers in Pune recorded the highest average sales per customer.
- Chennai and Bangalore also demonstrated strong customer spending behavior.

### Product Demand
The most consistently purchased products across major cities were:

- Cold Brew Coffee Pack (6 Bottles)
- Ground Espresso Coffee (250g)
- Instant Coffee Powder (100g)
- Coffee Beans (500g)

### Market Potential
Delhi has the largest estimated coffee-consuming population, indicating significant long-term growth potential.

### Cost Efficiency
Pune demonstrated one of the strongest combinations of:
- High revenue generation
- Strong customer spending
- Lower rent per customer

making it an attractive city for business expansion.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Expand Presence in Pune
Pune delivers the highest revenue while maintaining relatively low operational costs per customer.

### 2. Strengthen Operations in Chennai and Bangalore
Both cities demonstrate strong customer spending and consistent sales performance.

### 3. Target Delhi for Future Growth
Delhi offers the largest estimated coffee consumer market and significant long-term expansion potential.

### 4. Focus on Best-Selling Products
Increase inventory and promotional focus on:

- Cold Brew Coffee Pack
- Ground Espresso Coffee
- Instant Coffee Powder
- Coffee Beans

### 5. Monitor City-Level Sales Growth
Monthly growth analysis should be used to identify emerging high-potential markets.
